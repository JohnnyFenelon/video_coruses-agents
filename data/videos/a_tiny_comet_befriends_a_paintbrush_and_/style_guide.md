# Style Guide - cinematic_realism

Cinematic Realism: Photorealistic, 35mm film look, shallow depth of field, natural lighting, film grain, 24fps, anamorphic lens flares, color graded with teal-orange palette, ultra-detailed textures, ray-traced global illumination.

## Characters
- Leo: A child

## Visual Consistency Rules
- Maintain exact same character proportions across all shots
- Use consistent color palette (refer to character sheet)
- Keep lighting direction consistent within scenes
- Apply same post-processing grade across entire video
